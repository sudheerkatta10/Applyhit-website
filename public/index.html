FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you apply to jobs using my own accounts?

Yes. With your written consent, we apply through your job board profiles so recruiters contact you directly. Your login details are stored securely and never shared.

How do I know the applications are real?

Every application appears in your ApplyHit portal with the company, role, date and status as soon as it's submitted, and you'll receive confirmation emails from the employers directly.

Which roles and visa types do you support?

We focus on IT roles such as data engineering, analytics, machine learning, cloud and software development. We support a range of work authorizations, including OPT, STEM OPT, H‑1B, H4 EAD, green card holders and U.S. citizens, and match roles accordingly.

Can I pause my service?

Yes. If you need a break, for example while you're interviewing or have accepted an offer, let us know and we'll pause applications until you're ready to resume.
